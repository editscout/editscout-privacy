# EditScout Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 24, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

EditScout is a Chrome extension designed to help video editors analyze editing techniques, identify possible transitions and effects, and learn how to recreate them.

This Privacy Policy explains what information EditScout may access, how that information is used, and what choices users have.

## 2. Information We Access

Depending on the features you use, EditScout may access or process the following information:

- **Video information:** YouTube video URLs, video titles, video IDs, and timestamps associated with selected moments.
- **Video frames:** Frames or visual information from a selected video segment, where supported by the browser and the extension.
- **Saved references:** Titles, timestamps, tags, notes, analysis results, and other information you choose to save.
- **Extension settings:** Preferences needed to provide the extension's features.

EditScout is designed to process only the information needed to provide its functionality.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by EditScout may be used to:

- Analyze selected video moments for possible transitions, motion, visual effects, and other editing techniques.
- Generate editing suggestions and instructions.
- Save and organize references for later use.
- Remember user preferences and settings.
- Diagnose errors and improve functionality where applicable.

EditScout does not claim to recover the original creator's project files, exact effect settings, or editing workflow from a finished video.

## 4. Local Storage and External Services

EditScout may use Chrome's local storage or other browser storage mechanisms to retain saved references, settings, and analysis results.

**Local storage:** Information stored locally remains on your device unless you export it, synchronize it through another service, or a feature explicitly sends it elsewhere.

**External services:** If a feature uses an external AI provider, analysis service, or other third-party service, the information required by that feature may be transmitted to that provider and processed under its applicable terms and privacy policy.

The actual information transmitted depends on the features enabled and the services configured in the installed version of EditScout. Users should consult the extension's current feature descriptions for applicable limitations.

## 5. Data Sharing and Sale

EditScout does not sell personal information.

Information is not shared with third parties for their independent advertising purposes. If external services are used to provide analysis functionality, the information necessary for that functionality may be sent to the relevant service provider.

Any third-party processing is subject to the applicable provider's terms and privacy practices.

## 6. Permissions

EditScout requests browser permissions needed to provide its features, which may include interacting with supported YouTube pages, reading relevant video-page information, displaying the extension interface, and storing saved references and settings.

Permissions are used for the extension's stated functionality and should not be used to collect unrelated browsing information.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information saved locally can generally be removed by deleting saved references, clearing the extension's stored data, or uninstalling the extension.

Information transmitted to a third-party service, if applicable, may be subject to that provider's retention and deletion policies.

Exported files and copies saved outside the extension must be deleted separately by the user.

## 8. Children's Privacy

EditScout is a general-purpose video-editing utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

## 9. Security

Reasonable measures are used in the design of EditScout to limit access to information and reduce unnecessary data handling. However, no software or electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security.

Users should avoid entering confidential or sensitive information into notes or other fields unless necessary.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when EditScout's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this URL, along with an updated revision date.
